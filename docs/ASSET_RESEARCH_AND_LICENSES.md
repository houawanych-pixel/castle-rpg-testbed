# Asset research and license notes

The packaged prototype uses original/procedural drawing and therefore does not depend on third-party art downloads.

Researched replacement sources (all reported as CC0/public-domain style on their source pages at research time):
- OpenGameArt — "Pixel art top down dungeon tileset and rpg character with animations" by profpatonildo (CC0).
- OpenGameArt — "RPG Tilesets pack" by TheNess, 16×16 (CC0).
- OpenGameArt — "RPG Tileset" by russpuppy, 16×16 top-down fantasy set (CC0).
- OpenGameArt — "Top Down Dungeon Pack" by Screaming Brain Studios (CC0).
- OpenGameArt — "JRPG - End Dungeon" by HydroGene, loopable music (CC0).

Before redistributing a build with downloaded assets, retain a copy of each source page/license and verify the exact downloaded file matches the listed license.
