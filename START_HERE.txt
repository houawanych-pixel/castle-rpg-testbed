EVERTRAIL — THE NINEFOLD VAULT
Mobile landscape build • Godot 4.3 • September 23, 2026

ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE
1. Download this ZIP. In Samsung My Files, select it and choose Extract.
2. Extract into a new folder in Downloads or Documents.
3. Open Godot. Choose Import and select project.godot inside the extracted folder.
4. Allow Godot file access if Android asks. Wait for artwork/audio imports to finish.
5. Open the project, turn your phone sideways, and tap Run Project (the play triangle).
6. Tap NEW ADVENTURE. Later, use CONTINUE SAVED ADVENTURE.

This ZIP is editable Godot source, not an installable APK.
Use Godot 4.3 or newer; verified here with 4.3. Compatibility renderer is configured.
Landscape rotation is requested. The 16:9 canvas keeps its proportions on wider phones.

CONTROLS
Left circular stick: move. SWORD: attack. Hold GUARD: shield in your facing direction.
RANG/BOW: starting items. MENU: equipment and pause. MAP: your routes.
TALK: nearby villagers, shop refill, or a room hint.
In MENU, tap any equipment tile, then the A or B assignment box.
RANG means boomerang. LAMP means lantern.
Movement and the right-hand buttons support simultaneous fingers.

FIRST OBJECTIVE
Walk east from the village, east through the meadow, east through the stone approach.
You have reached the Ninefold Vault. Each three-room wing contains a puzzle, a key
seal and a guardian. Light both braziers with your lantern/fire rod, or push the
stone onto the gold plate. Treasure chests supply keys. Defeat each guardian.
You can retreat west. Defeat the ninth guardian to complete the adventure.

HELPFUL DETAILS
Visit the village's red-roof shop and tap TALK to restore health and supplies.
Swim fins are in Sunken Steps, south of Mosswater Shore. Follow dry land around water.
Magic slowly regenerates, so elemental fights cannot run out of required magic.
Prism Regent's blue ring needs ICE; its red ring needs FIRE.
Progress saves on room changes, menu use, focus loss, and every 15 seconds of play.
Save + Resume also saves. New Adventure starts over and replaces this build's save.
Save files stay in Godot's user-data folder, not inside this ZIP.

SCOPE
An original 36-room action-RPG prototype: 9 exploration rooms and 27 vault rooms,
9 guardian encounters, original music/SFX, new detailed sprite artwork, touch
controls, inventory, route map, persistent props and saving. It is not Nintendo's
full game, maps, story or exact mechanics. Bosses share some art; NPC animation is
simple. Device performance and touch comfort still need testing on your phone.
